SoftBridge Authenticator

Security simplified. Organized protection.

Protect your digital life with SoftBridge Authenticator, a powerful two-factor authentication (2FA) tool designed to keep your accounts safe while keeping you organized. Part of the SoftBridge 365 ecosystem, it offers seamless synchronization and high-grade encryption across all your devices.

Smart Organization

Use folders to categorize your codes. Keep your work, social media, and financial accounts in separate, easy-to-find spaces.

Full Customization

Edit account details with ease. Update service names, usernames, and even add custom icons or notes to keep your list looking exactly how you want it.

TOTP Export/Import

Never lose access to your accounts. Easy Export and Import TOTP Codes from any device with SoftBridge Auth backup files.

Cloud Support (SB 365)

Unlock end-to-end encrypted backup, access to all premium SoftBridge Labs products, and lightning-fast restores via SoftBridge Sync.

Privacy Screen

Built-in security that restricts screenshots and screen recording to ensure your codes remain visible only to you.

Simple and Reliable

Fast setup and a lightweight experience built for maximum security without the complexity.
