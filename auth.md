# Agent Authentication & Registration Policy

All resources, product data feeds, and developer utilities provided on this website are publicly accessible and read-only. 

## Authentication Policy
AI Agents do not require registration or authentication to crawl or access the public data feeds of SoftBridge Labs.

### Public Endpoints
- **Ecosystem Catalog:** `https://softbridgelabs.in/data/products.json`
- **Dev Tools Catalog:** `https://softbridgelabs.in/data/devtools.json`
- **Careers Catalog:** `https://softbridgelabs.in/data/jobs.json`

If your agent requires OAuth or registered credentials for specific client integrations, please contact our support team at `support@softbridgelabs.in`.
