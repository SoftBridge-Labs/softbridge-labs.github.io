Campus Ambassador

Community & Growth

We're looking for enthusiastic students to represent SoftBridge Labs at their colleges and universities. As a Campus Ambassador, you'll introduce fellow students to our apps, tools, and services — and help us build a strong community of young developers and creators. You'll earn rewards, recognition, and real-world experience in marketing and community building.

What You'll Do

  • Promote SoftBridge Labs apps and services within your campus community
  • Organize or participate in tech events, workshops, and demos
  • Share SoftBridge Labs content on social media and college groups
  • Refer students and potential clients to our services
  • Provide feedback from your campus community to our team
  • Represent SoftBridge Labs at college fests and hackathons

Who We're Looking For

  • Currently enrolled in a college or university
  • Active presence on social media and college networks
  • Passion for technology, software, and startups
  • Good communication and networking skills
  • Self-driven with the ability to work independently

What You Get

  • Official SoftBridge Labs Campus Ambassador certificate
  • Performance-based commissions and rewards
  • Exclusive SoftBridge Labs swag and merchandise
  • Letter of recommendation from the founding team
  • Priority consideration for full-time roles after graduation
  • Access to internal tools and early product releases

Ready to Represent?

Tell us your college, your social reach, and why you'd be a great ambassador for SoftBridge Labs.
