We're looking for a motivated Sales Intern to join our Growth team. Your primary role is to identify potential clients and bring them to SoftBridge Labs for our services — web development, Android development, app testing, logo design, and video editing. There's no fixed salary; you earn a competitive commission for every successful client conversion. This is a great opportunity to build real sales experience, grow your network, and earn on your own terms.

What You'll Do

  • Identify and reach out to potential clients via LinkedIn, social media, and personal networks
  • Pitch SoftBridge Labs services to businesses and individuals
  • Follow up with leads and coordinate with our internal team
  • Maintain a simple log of outreach and conversions
  • Represent SoftBridge Labs professionally in all communications

What We're Looking For

  • Strong communication and persuasion skills
  • Self-motivated with a results-driven mindset
  • Basic understanding of software/tech services is a plus
  • Ability to work independently with minimal supervision
  • No prior experience required — just hustle and drive

What You Get

  • Flexible remote work — work from anywhere, anytime
  • Performance-based earning with no cap on commissions
  • Certificate of internship on successful completion
  • Direct mentorship from the founding team
  • Opportunity to convert to a full-time role based on performance

How the Earning Works

You bring a client to us — we close the deal and deliver the project. You earn a commission on the project value. The more clients you bring, the more you earn. There's no ceiling. We'll share the exact commission structure during onboarding.
